# CUAD attribution

The optional offline evaluation workflow uses the Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset (CUAD) v1, created and maintained by The Atticus Project.

- Dataset: https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad/
- Source repository: https://github.com/The-Atticus-Project/cuad
- Archived release: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4595826
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)

Citation:

> Dan Hendrycks, Collin Burns, Anya Chen, and Spencer Ball. "CUAD: An Expert-Annotated NLP Dataset for Legal Contract Review." NeurIPS, 2021.

CUAD and its labeling handbook are reference and evaluation sources. The Atticus Project does not sponsor or endorse Calder Agreement Review. Review the dataset disclaimer and the license status of underlying EDGAR contracts before redistributing the corpus.
