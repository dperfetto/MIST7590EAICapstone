# Bonterms and Common Paper attribution

The Section 6.3 independent check (`evaluation/independent-check/`) uses standard-form
agreements published by Bonterms and Common Paper as its unmodified source material, per the
project brief's Section 7.2. Both publishers license their agreements under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).

## Bonterms

- Publisher: https://bonterms.com/
- Source repositories: https://github.com/Bonterms
- Documents used: Cloud Terms v1.0, Professional Services Agreement v1.2, Data Protection
  Addendum v1.0, Mutual NDA v1.0, Service Level Agreement v1.0
- License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)

## Common Paper

- Publisher: https://commonpaper.com/
- Source repositories: https://github.com/CommonPaper
- Documents used: Cloud Service Agreement (CSA), Professional Services Agreement (PSA), Data
  Protection Addendum (DPA), Mutual NDA, Software License Agreement
- License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)

## How they are used here

`evaluation/independent-check/originals/` holds the unmodified text of each document, pulled
directly from the publisher's GitHub repository. `evaluation/independent-check/modified/` holds
team-authored derivative edits of those originals — a modification permitted by CC BY 4.0,
attributed here. Neither Bonterms nor Common Paper sponsors or endorses Calder Agreement Review,
and the modified versions do not represent either publisher's recommended or standard language;
they were deliberately altered to test Calder's presence detector, per the project brief's
Section 6.3.
